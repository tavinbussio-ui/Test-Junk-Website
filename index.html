Craftsmanship in Every Cut

Old-school service, clean modern cuts, and the finish customers remember.

Specialties

Men's and boys' haircuts finished with a warm lather shave around the ears and neck. Fades, flat tops, and modern styles are all part of the chair.

History

Lou and Cherie left the B.Y.U. Barbershop to buy the Payson shop in 1996, bringing decades of barbering experience to Main Street.

Meet Louis B.

Lou and Cherie both attended barber college and have each been barbers for over 35 years. They live in Payson and love this community.

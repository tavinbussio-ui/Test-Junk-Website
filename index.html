About Us

Lou and Cherie bought the Payson shop in 1996 after working at the B.Y.U. Barbershop. They are traditional barbers with over 35 years of experience each, proudly serving Payson since 1996.

Meet Louis B.

Lou and Cherie both attended barber college, Lou in Salt Lake City and Cherie in California. They live in Payson and keep the shop focused on friendly, finished-looking cuts.

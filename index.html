Traditional Barbershop

Classic cuts, warm lather finish, familiar Main Street service.

Specialties

Men's and boys' haircuts finished with a warm lather shave around the ears and neck. The shop does a lot of fades, flat tops, and modern styles.

History

Established in 1996 after Lou and Cherie left the B.Y.U. Barbershop to buy the shop in Payson.

Meet Louis B.

Lou and Cherie both attended barber college and have each been barbers for over 35 years. They live in Payson and love the community.
